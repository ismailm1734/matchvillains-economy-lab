The core trade-off

sweep wall difficulty →

Harder walls squeeze more revenue per player — until frustration churns enough players that total revenue falls. The marked point is the balance sweet spot.

Revenue / player End retention Sweet spot
How it's modelled — a 1,000-player cohort is walked level by level. Match Villains is IAP-only, so there are no ads. On each level some players fail; a loss is either a near win (out of moves just short — the moment the game offers extra moves) or a far-off loss, and near-wins convert far more often. Whoever doesn't buy extra moves leans on lives: burn them all and you hit a gate — pay to refill, wait, or quit. Churn concentrates at that gate, and walls make it worse. The numbers are a designer's back-of-envelope model, not live studio data — the point is the shape, not a forecast.
